## Service comparison
A: hosting target 99.9% per defined month; initial response within four support hours, weekdays 9 am–5 pm; export available.
B: hosting target 99.95% per defined month; initial response within two hours, 24-hour support; export conditions unconfirmed.
These fictional extracts omit other terms. Do not invent missing scope, measurement, exclusions, prices or remedies.
- Compare scope, availability, support, export and responsibilities.
- Calculate the illustrative unavailable minutes in a 30-day model and explain the limits.
- Identify the evidence needed before selecting a service.
