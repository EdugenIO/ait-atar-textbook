# Teaching sequence decisions

16 October 2026: the user requested four 55-minute lessons per week for 16 teaching weeks for each unit, plus two weeks for student examination time. The CS companion's sequence uses 16 teaching weeks followed by two blocked exam weeks per unit; this is the adopted interpretation for AIT.

## Allocation

| Scope | Teaching weeks | Teaching lessons | Teaching minutes | Exam weeks | Total planning weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One unit | 16 | 64 | 3,520 | 2 | 18 |
| One year, two units | 32 | 128 | 7,040 | 4 | 36 |
| Two years, four units | 64 | 256 | 14,080 | 8 | 72 |

Teaching time per unit is 58 hours 40 minutes. The school's actual examination duration is not calculated from lesson periods. Exam weeks are reserved calendar time and have no assigned new teaching. Project work, feedback, consolidation and timed practice occupy the scheduled teaching periods; they are not extra lessons.

## Teaching design

- A named task for each of the four periods each week.
- Weekly evidence developed across the four in-class sessions.
- Chapter references for all 20 content-area destinations.
- A default 5/10/10/25/5-minute lesson structure, with 55-minute workshop and timed-practice alternatives.
- Weeks 15–16 consolidate work and prepare students for the exam window.
- All session plans are explicitly labelled Planned. Chapter resource status is separate.
- Schools can move the exam windows to their calendar without changing the order of teaching weeks. No fixed dates or term boundaries are assumed.
- The sequence is a draft recommendation, not a formal school assessment outline or a completed syllabus audit.
