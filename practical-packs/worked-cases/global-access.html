Compare historical online access restrictions

Unit 4 impacts · Historical snapshots concerning Wikipedia access. This case makes no claim about present-day availability in either jurisdiction.

Sources checked 27 October 2026; review by 27 April 2027 and before classroom reuse. The Foundation is the affected publisher and an advocate, not a neutral measurement service.

Two dated primary accounts

In its 17 May 2019 statement on China, Wikimedia reported a block affecting all Wikipedia language editions. This supports a dated account of the publisher’s observation and position. It does not establish today’s reachability, every network’s behaviour or the authorities’ full reasoning.

In its 15 January 2020 statement on Turkey, Wikimedia reported access restoration following the Constitutional Court’s 26 December 2019 decision. Its note says restoration varied between providers at that point. A judgment, an announcement and network restoration are different events.

Worked comparison and consequence

Both snapshots concern access to a whole knowledge service across language editions. The Turkey account includes a documented legal challenge and reports of restoration; the China statement describes the publisher’s opposition to a block at its publication date. The documents have different dates and purposes, so do not turn them into a current ranking of countries.

A fictional international learning provider should avoid making one external service the only route to essential instructions. Provide a permitted accessible offline/text alternative and verify delivery in intended contexts. Consider authors’ ability to contribute and learners’ access to knowledge, as well as availability. This is a continuity recommendation inferred from the case, not a legal instruction to evade restrictions.

Guided practice

Does the 2020 restoration announcement prove every learner in Turkey had access that day?

Compare reasoning

No. The publisher’s own qualification describes differences between providers. Record source, date and scope; a broad headline does not establish every user’s experience.

Independent transfer

Choose a teacher-approved dated access-restriction case. Compare a primary decision/authority statement with an affected party’s account, identify their interests, and separate event dates from publication dates. Explain one impact on collaboration or knowledge access, give a justified delivery response and state what the evidence cannot establish.
