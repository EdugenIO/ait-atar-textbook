Compare a documented mobile-device trend

Unit 3 hardware · Historical comparison of the Wi-Fi 11-inch iPad Pro introduced in 2022 and the Wi-Fi 11-inch M4 model introduced in 2024. This is a bounded trend case, not a claim that either is the latest or a purchase recommendation.

Source check: 27 October 2026. Review before reuse and by 27 April 2027. The client brief below is fictional; listed specifications come from Apple.

Primary specifications: Apple 2022 model and Apple 2024 model. Model introduction years are given on those pages; page revision dates are not treated as introduction dates. Compare Wi-Fi with Wi-Fi, and SDR with SDR. HDR peak brightness is a different condition.

Worked client judgement

A fictional festival team needs a readable offline schedule during a four-hour walking shift and already owns second-generation Pencils. The later model is 22 g lighter, about 4.7% of 466 g, and has a higher stated SDR brightness limit. Those changes may help carrying and viewing. They do not establish outdoor readability, four-hour runtime or supported offline behaviour.

The owned second-generation Pencil is not listed as compatible with the M4 model; account for accessories rather than assuming a newer device meets the workflow. Run a representative offline schedule, glare and battery trial, check the required app/OS version and obtain actual costs. Keep the decision conditional while those facts are unknown.

Guided practice

Why is “1000 is greater than 600, therefore the later model is the correct purchase” unsupported?

Compare reasoning

Brightness is one specification with stated conditions. App support, glare, input needs, battery use, accessories and budget remain relevant. A requirement gate comes before ranking optional improvements.

Independent transfer

Compare one approved contemporary mobile device with a desktop workflow using dated primary specifications. Record essentials as pass/fail/unknown, distinguish supplier claims from measurements, and propose a task test. Include an access date and review trigger; do not use clock speed alone to rank usability.
