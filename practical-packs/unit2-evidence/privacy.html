Follow personal information from collection to disposal

Unit 2 · fictional bicycle-shop client brief. Read the chapter explanation first. These are worked decisions, not observed learner results.

Worked decisions

FocusDecision and explanation
Coverage firstCheck whether the organisation/activity is covered by the Privacy Act; small-business exemptions have exceptions. Responsible practice still matters when an exemption applies.
CollectionA fictional repair booking needs an item description and approved contact method; a date of birth is not justified merely because a form allows it.
Use/accessUsing a contact detail for a booking is a different decision from publishing it. Staff require authorised access; a person’s request to access their information requires the applicable identity/scope procedure.
Identity theftUnexpected collection or disclosure can support impersonation. Reduce unnecessary data, restrict access and use an approved verification process rather than exposing a record in a public chat.
DisposalSet a justified retention point, inspect backups/shared copies and use an approved disposal method. Deleting a visible shortcut does not establish that all copies were removed.
Digital divideA device alone does not remove cost, connectivity, skill, language, accessibility or app-availability barriers. Offer an approved alternative and reconcile its bookings with the same record.

Primary guidance, checked 27 October 2026: OAIC small-business coverage. Recheck before classroom issue; this is a teaching model, not a legal determination.

Apply independently

Review a fictional booking specification. Justify each field, describe authorised use, distinguish staff access from an individual access request, identify an impersonation risk and design a retention/disposal record. Evaluate resource, skill and web-app access barriers, with a coordinated fallback.

Use this within the existing chapter task, not an extra assessed project or additional teaching week. Record expected and actual results separately.

Evidence to retain

  • Use invented data and record Privacy Act coverage as a checked question.
  • Avoid assuming no names means anonymous data.
  • Give a reason and check for each stage of the information lifecycle.
