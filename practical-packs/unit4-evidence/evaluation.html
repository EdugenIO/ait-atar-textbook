Check your reasoning

Mean ease increased. Does that alone prove every learner completed the task?

Compare your explanation

No. The rating and task completion are different measures; inspect completion records and unresolved barriers.

Apply independently

Work through these steps for your own version of the community-centre brief. Retain your own output and actual observations, separately from the supplied model.

  1. Define your own fictional client task, acceptance criterion, project schedule and representative prototype, then build it using approved tools.
  2. Inspect the existing before/after prototypes and recalculate the supplied data before reading the model interpretation.
  3. Design a short survey, client-feedback prompt and self-reflection question that answer different evaluation questions. Collect only teacher-approved evidence with appropriate handling.
  4. Use actual findings to implement a revision, repeat the relevant check and prepare a qualified handover. Keep your real observations separate from the supplied invented model.

Use this within the existing chapter task, not an extra assessed project or additional teaching week. Record expected and actual results separately.

Evidence to retain

  • Brief, plan, produced source/output and actual progress/change record.
  • Separate survey, client feedback, reflection and task observations.
  • A traceable implemented revision/retest plus sample/order/environment limits.

Actual learner performance, native application output and independent teacher review remain unverified. Supplied model results must not be copied into an observed-result log.
