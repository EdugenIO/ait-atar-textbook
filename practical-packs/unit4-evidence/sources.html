Resolve source conflicts before publishing

Unit 4 · fictional community-centre client brief. Read the chapter explanation first. These are worked decisions, not observed learner results.

Worked decisions

FocusDecision and explanation
Worked evidence choiceThe existing five-source fictional case has an older approved schedule, a current approved revision, a newer stale repost, an unsupported supplier claim and an unapproved draft. The approved revision is the basis for the current venue decision; the newest timestamp alone does not decide it.
VerifiabilityTrace authorship, original record, approval and the exact claim. A source can be verifiably a supplier’s statement while its claimed productivity gain remains unsupported.
AccuracyCompare the claim with relevant evidence and resolve discrepancies. A professional layout, HTTPS connection or repeated repost does not verify a venue or causal conclusion.
CurrencyConsider publication/access dates, version and whether a record is superseded or approved. A high revision number on an unapproved draft does not make it the current published instruction.

Apply independently

  3. Evaluate one teacher-approved claim using two primary sources. Record exact claim, dates, evidence, discrepancies, decision and remaining uncertainties.
