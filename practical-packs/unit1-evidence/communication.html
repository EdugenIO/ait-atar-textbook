Worked decisions

Fictional repair café brief: residents must find what to bring, the date, venue and next action. The decisions below model reasoning; they are not observed learner results.

FocusDecision and explanation
Social networking and featuresA profile/feed service supports sharing and visible interactions; compare intended audience, persistence, privacy controls and moderation.
Online chat roomReal-time text supports quick coordination but decisions can be hard to recover; keep a separate decision summary and agree response windows.
Virtual worldAvatars interact in a shared simulated environment; check controls, access requirements, audience and moderation rather than assuming anonymity means safety.
Responsible citizenshipUse consent-aware sharing, accurate source credit, respectful messages and agreed boundaries; a private group can still be reshared.
Forms of bullyingFictional threats, impersonation, exclusion and humiliation require a response; do not recreate harmful posts for the task.
Limit and respond to harmDo not amplify it; seek trusted adult support, use appropriate evidence/reporting/blocking guidance and keep intimate-image material out of class evidence.
Work–life balanceAgree an ordinary response window such as 4–6 pm on school days with an approved urgent-help route; review whether this excludes team members.

Current eSafety guidance. Use invented scenarios, not real harmful posts.

Apply independently

Compare a moderated forum, chat room, visual feed and virtual world for a fictional group project. Choose one, justify its features and limits, and write a communication agreement with response windows, moderation and a safe route for a fictional harm concern.

Use this as part of the existing chapter task, not an additional assessed project or extra lesson. Predict outcomes, create/inspect the required output and record the actual result.

Evidence to retain

  • Define the tool’s actual interaction features, not only its brand.
  • Discuss access, visibility, persistence and time boundaries.
  • Use invented scenarios and current eSafety guidance.
