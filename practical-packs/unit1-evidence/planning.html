Worked decisions

Fictional repair café brief: residents must find what to bring, the date, venue and next action. The decisions below model reasoning; they are not observed learner results.

FocusDecision and explanation
Purpose and criteriaHelp residents decide to attend; check whether date, venue and next action can be located at the intended output size.
AudienceConsider residents’ reading, access and viewing contexts; the client and audience are not automatically the same people.
MediumA phone image and noticeboard print need different size/rendering checks.
Situation analysisThe previous notice hides the venue; identify the evidence, constraints and missing information before proposing changes.
Style guideRecord heading/body treatments, colour roles, spacing, alignment and image treatment with reasons.
StoryboardThree panels show the sequence: event → what to bring → venue/time, with narration and timing notes.
ThumbnailsTwo small layouts compare arrangement and hierarchy before detailed production.
Wireframe/sketchA labelled structure locates heading, details, action and return route if interactive; a static poster does not need invented controls.
EvaluationFor each criterion, record the actual observation, revision, retest and limitation. An attractive output does not prove the venue task works.

Editable poster · Chapter project task.

Apply independently

Produce a short client brief, situation analysis, audience/medium decision, criteria table and style guide. Sketch two thumbnails, storyboard the three-scene version and wireframe an optional linked page. Build one appropriate digital output with annotations covering elements/principles, typography, composition, thirds and grid/alignment. Test the brief’s tasks, revise and evaluate with evidence.

Use this as part of the existing chapter task, not an additional assessed project or extra lesson. Predict outcomes, create/inspect the required output and record the actual result.

Evidence to retain

  • Retain representations with different purposes; hand/digital sketches are both acceptable.
  • Apply the design process to an actual output rather than stopping at a plan.
  • Record every evaluation criterion separately and identify untested claims.

Teacher/classroom performance is unverified until the learner actually performs the task. No SCSA approval is implied.
