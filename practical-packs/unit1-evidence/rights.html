Worked decisions

Fictional repair café brief: residents must find what to bring, the date, venue and next action. The decisions below model reasoning; they are not observed learner results.

FocusDecision and explanation
Copyright purposeCheck control of the proposed reproduction/publication of a protected image; merely finding it online does not authorise reuse.
Fair dealingResearch/study and criticism/review are specified purposes with conditions; a public promotion is not automatically permitted because it began as school work.
Private useA lawful personal-use copy and a public upload are different uses; private copying provisions have limited conditions. Do not treat private use as blanket permission.
Moral rightsKeep creator credit accurate, avoid false attribution and consider integrity; copyright ownership and creator rights are not identical.
Reference and IP acknowledgementFictional model: Mo Example, Repair icon, supplied class asset, permission for classroom mock-up only, accessed 27 October 2026, cropped. This is a model record, not a real licence. Include source/terms and required credit in the output, not only a private log.

Primary guidance: Attorney-General: copyright and moral rights. Check the applicable exception and terms for the intended use.
