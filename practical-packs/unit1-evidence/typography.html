Make type and composition decisions measurable

Unit 1 · supplementary worked evidence. Read the chapter explanation first.

Worked decisions

Fictional repair café brief: residents must find what to bring, the date, venue and next action. The decisions below model reasoning; they are not observed learner results.

FocusDecision and explanation
TypefaceUse one readable sans-serif family for practical details; a different family needs a communication reason.
SizeSet heading 40, details 22 and supporting copy 18 in the editable example; test the delivered size rather than treating these values as universal.
AlignmentAlign date and venue to a shared left edge at x=64; inspect accidental near-alignments.
FormatUse bold on labels and regular body copy to distinguish roles; excessive bolding removes that difference.
SpacingKeep a larger gap between information groups than within a date/venue group.
Reading gravityFor this English-language poster, trial a top-to-bottom/left-to-right path; imagery, script direction and audience can change it.
ThirdsIn a 900 × 600 layout, thirds lie at x=300/600 and y=200/400; explore a focal point there, then justify any alternative.
Grid and alignmentUse a 64-pixel margin and shared edges; grid guides are planning aids, not mandatory visible artwork.

Apply independently

Open the editable poster. Create a phone-viewed version and a noticeboard version. Annotate typeface, size, alignment, formatting, spacing, reading path, thirds and grid. Explain which decisions transfer and which need a new output-size test.

Use this as part of the existing chapter task, not an additional assessed project or extra lesson. Predict outcomes, create/inspect the required output and record the actual result.

Evidence to retain

  • Record numeric positions/sizes where useful and explain their purpose.
  • Create accurate layouts rather than only describing a proposed design.
  • Retest the date, venue and next action at the intended viewing size.
