Production sequence

  1. Define two essential field criteria and two production criteria, including user/context and how each will be checked.
  2. Create an A/B/C table with pass, fail and unverified against the supplied fictional data. Explain at least two specification-to-task relationships.
  3. Use the dated trend case, then find a current primary source in an approved research session. Record publication/access dates, exact claim and limits; identify what is actually changing over time.
  4. Recommend roles or a combined workflow, state missing evidence, propose a representative trial and a fallback. No purchase or new account is required.

Apply independently

Complete the production sequence using your own version of the festival brief. Change the venue and one user access need, then justify and test the resulting revision. Retain your own outputs and observations; the supplied reference is a model.

Use this within the existing chapter task, not an extra assessed project or additional teaching week. Record expected and actual results separately.

Evidence to retain

  • Separate fictional model data, manufacturer statements and your actual observations.
  • Record dates and conditions for any real device comparison.
  • Retain failed essentials and unknowns rather than a single unexplained score.

Actual learner performance, native application output and independent teacher review remain unverified. Supplied model results must not be copied into an observed-result log.
