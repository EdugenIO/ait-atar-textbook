Build and compare motion

Pack guide

Press Play to move the wheel from left to right over two seconds. Then edit the positions or compare with the clearly labelled stationary exercise. Nothing plays automatically.

Frame mode holds each specified position for a frame. Tween mode calculates intermediate positions from the first and last values; it does not use the 22 middle positions. Both run for two seconds, but may look different.
