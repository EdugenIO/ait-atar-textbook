## What the statuses mean

- **Knowledge leaves:** authored explanation/model/task located; independent subject review pending.
- **Skill leaves:** authored task/model located; actual learner performance and output unverified.
- **Parent rows:** context only; inspect the linked children and relevant task.

An authored task does not demonstrate that a student used software or achieved a standard. A software-change plan does not mean an installer ran. A source log does not authorise an unclear use. A single layout/user trial does not establish accessibility or universal success. Modern clarifications such as macOS and iPadOS do not remove the syllabus's named Mac OS/iOS examples.

Selected rights/safety guidance was checked against [Attorney-General copyright-owner guidance](https://www.ag.gov.au/rights-and-protections/copyright/copyright-owners) and [eSafety young-person guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/young-people/cyberbullying).
