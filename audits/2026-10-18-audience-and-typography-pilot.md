# Student audience and teacher reference: typography pilot

Primary reader: a 16- or 17-year-old studying AIT ATAR. Secondary reader: a teacher looking for accurate explanations, curriculum context and adaptable classroom activities.

## Editorial approach

Use direct instructions and familiar situations without talking down to the reader. Keep subject vocabulary, define it beside a concrete example, and separate an observation from an assumption. Use short paragraphs with a single purpose. Activities should invite prediction, exploration and explanation without marks, accounts or tracking. Avoid adding the same interaction or question template to every topic.

Teacher reference belongs in clearly labelled optional notes. Explain the learning purpose, relevant terminology, likely misconceptions, possible discussion and adaptations. Keep current syllabus links and the draft-review status visible. Distinguish a suggested classroom activity from validated evidence of improved learning. Student text must remain understandable without opening teacher notes.
