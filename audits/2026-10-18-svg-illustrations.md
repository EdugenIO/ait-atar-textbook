# Standalone SVG lesson illustrations — edition 0.27

Created 12 original, self-contained 900 × 540 SVG images: three for each unit. They replace selected inline diagrams or tables in the existing contextual figure placements rather than creating a second repeated visual block.

Unit 1: working memory/saved files; exact-match lookup row; observe/revise/retest cycle.
Unit 2: master/delivery branches; physical star/service roles; responsive reflow.
Unit 3: content hierarchy and return routes; scheme/profile/output distinction; change-request impacts.
Unit 4: warehouse/mart and hosting axes; browser/receiving-side validation; staff/visitor access boundary.

Images have accessible titles and descriptions, and lesson images have meaningful alternatives. Labels and text explain meaning without relying on colour alone. Complex diagrams retain a labelled keyboard-scrollable region at narrow widths and an open-full-size link. A public illustration library supplies individual SVG downloads and links to the related chapters. Its route is assets/diagrams/index.html.

Both student and answer workbook copies embed the SVG bytes so the images remain available offline. No external fonts, scripts, raster dependencies or remote resources are required by the SVG files. The original diagrams remain scalable and editable for teacher reuse under the repository terms.

Domain checks include matching worksheet columns/range/return value, bidirectional physical LAN links, separate validation/identity/permission claims, and the distinct scope-versus-hosting axes. Illustrative network boundaries do not claim a verified configuration. These visual additions do not close outstanding practical-operation requirements from the syllabus audit.

Checks cover all 12 assets and gallery links, all four units, topic placement and exact offline embedding. Browser visual checks sample the gallery and key complex illustrations before publication.
