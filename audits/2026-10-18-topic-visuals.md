# Contextual topic visuals — edition 0.26

Added 24 targeted explanatory figures, one per authored chapter, across Units 1–4. These complement the 48 existing section diagrams and the separate chapter walkthroughs. The new placements avoid topics already containing a section diagram.

Each figure explains a specific relationship beside the corresponding paragraph. Formats vary with the concept: annotated spreadsheet, tone swatches, composition grid, visual-weight analogy, memory/file workflow, LAN sketch, responsive layout, content tree, process sequence, boundary-value table and evidence comparisons. Short labels, captions and descriptions support Years 11–12 readers without removing technical terminology.

The same figures appear in focused topic pages, complete chapter views and both printable workbook versions. These are teaching explanations, not hidden practice solutions. SVGs have descriptive titles/text alternatives; diagrams and wide tables have labelled keyboard-focusable scroll regions. Explanatory captions remain outside the scrolling graphic. Swatches retain text labels and print colour settings; colour is not the only carrier of meaning.

Accuracy controls: fictional examples are labelled or contextualised; the spreadsheet search/return and range labels match the existing example; the form table uses the existing required whole-number 1–12 rule. LAN sketches distinguish a proposal from verified configuration. Colour-management and output comparisons do not claim universal settings or accessibility certification.

Verification checks the 24 placements, non-collision with existing diagrams, SVG alternatives, print inclusion and selected domain examples. Visual browser review samples the spreadsheet, hardware and network figures at desktop/narrow widths. Existing publication checks remain required.

This release improves explanatory support. It does not close the major syllabus audit's practical-operation gaps or establish independent curriculum sign-off. Additional topic illustrations should be chosen for a learning purpose, not generated merely to fill every page.
