2. Understand the lesson ideas

A digital solution needs suitable services, a manageable plan and evidence that it meets the brief. Read service commitments carefully, compare delivery options and use feedback to improve the result.

Interpret availability without turning it into a guarantee

For a simplified 30-day period, there are 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 minutes. A 99.9% target corresponds to 0.1% unavailable time: 43,200 × 0.001 = 43.2 minutes. A 99.95% target corresponds to 21.6 minutes under the same assumptions. These figures are mathematical allowances in an illustrative model, not predictions or guaranteed maximum outage lengths. Real agreements define measurement, exclusions and remedies. A target for one service component does not automatically cover the whole user journey.

Explore this concept and its visual/example support

Separate support response from service restoration

A provider may promise an initial response within a stated period during specified support hours. That does not mean every fault will be repaired in the same period. Check severity definitions, reporting channels, escalation and responsibilities. A service may be technically available while an application task is broken. Decide which service and support evidence matters to the centre’s hours of operation and the consequences of interruption.

Language you can use in your explanation
Service level agreement
An agreement defining a service’s scope, expected levels and relevant responsibilities and conditions.
Service scope
The functions, resources or support included in a service arrangement.
Availability target
A stated level of service availability over a defined measurement period and under stated conditions.
Support response time
The time within which a provider is expected to respond under specified support conditions; it is not necessarily the time to fix the issue.
Acceptance criterion
An agreed observable condition used to decide whether a deliverable meets a requirement.
Survey
A structured set of questions used to gather responses from a defined group.

3. Follow a worked decision

A fictional community centre needs reliable activity information, a manageable online learning resource and a justified service/network proposal. Separate source approval, personal-information handling, actual operation and intended design.

Today’s decision in that case

  1. Identify: Which requirement, concept or distinction does this decision address?
  2. Explain: Which part of the reasoning supports the decision?
  3. Qualify: What condition or evidence would you need before claiming it works in practice?

Deeper chapter model: Choose a service and evaluate the centre guide
  1. Client needs: The fictional centre wants the public guide available for evening activities, useful support arrangements and an export route. Confirm which criteria are essential.
  2. Sample A: Hosting target 99.9% per defined month; initial support response within four support hours, weekdays 9 am–5 pm; export available. Other terms are not supplied.
  3. Sample B: Hosting target 99.95% per defined month; initial support response within two hours, with 24-hour support; export conditions unconfirmed. Other terms are not supplied.
  4. Comparison: B has the higher stated hosting target and broader support in these fictional extracts. It still has an export evidence gap. Neither excerpt establishes full scope, exclusions, cost, restoration time or actual performance.
  5. Prototype observation: In an invented trial, a visitor finds the venue but cannot reach the form check button by keyboard. The client likes the layout.
  6. Revision and retest: Fix the keyboard barrier, preserve the useful layout and repeat the form task and related checks. Keep the original failure record.
  7. Qualified judgement: Do not report full acceptance until essential service questions and the relevant failed or untested criteria are resolved. State the limited evidence supplied.

Service comparison and product evaluation are connected but separate. A higher availability target cannot cancel a missing essential feature or a failed user task.

Spot and correct a connected misconception

Claim to examine: A two-hour support response means the service will always be repaired within two hours.

Compare the correction

Response and restoration are different commitments. Check the defined support conditions and any restoration commitment instead of inventing one.

Supported chapter question

Sample B has the higher availability target, but the client requires export and its export conditions are unknown. Can you mark B as meeting every essential requirement?

Check the supported reasoning

No. Record the export requirement as unverified and obtain suitable evidence. Compare the complete relevant terms and actual task behaviour before making an unconditional recommendation.

Related practice and explanation

Distinguish

Why should service type be checked before comparing targets?

Compare an explained answer

Targets may cover different components or scopes. Compare like-for-like service commitments and the complete user task.

Evaluate

A client approves the colours but a keyboard task fails. Is the product fully accepted against that task criterion?

Compare an explained answer

No. Positive visual feedback does not repair the interaction failure. Revise and retest the failed criterion.

Transfer and test the limits

Change one relevant client condition: audience/access need, output medium, available application, recovery need or resource limit. Explain which part of today’s decision still applies, which part changes and what new evidence you need. Choose a change that matters to this lesson rather than adding an unrelated feature.

Teaching choices and review limits

Use retrieval, explicit instruction, a worked decision, faded support, self-explanation, discussion, independent application, feedback and reflection in the core route. Optional error analysis, interleaving and transfer can replace practice when appropriate. For a learner needing support, narrow the task and retain one scaffold; for a learner ready for challenge, remove the scaffold or vary a meaningful condition. No new accounts, real personal data or purchases are required.

The lesson uses selected chapter readings and a distinct scheduled-task model. Native application operation, approved online collection, representative access trials and independent subject review are not established by the supplied text. Formal assessment follows the school’s approved conditions.
