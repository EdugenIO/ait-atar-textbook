STUDENT LESSON PAGES

Your lessons, week by week

Four 55-minute lessons each week. Read, reason, practise and apply the ideas on a page for that specific lesson.

Each unit has 16 teaching weeks and 64 lessons. Weeks 17–18 remain school exam reserves. The reference chapters stay available for deeper reading and revision.

Unit 1 · Year 11

Reference chapters · Teacher sequence

Week 1: Audience, purpose and the design brief 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Diagnose prior knowledge and identify the purpose of a community campaign. · Lesson 1
  2. Describe the target audience and turn its needs into design criteria. · Lesson 2
  3. Compare two digital products against the same audience needs. · Lesson 3
  4. Write a short client brief and explain how success will be judged. · Lesson 4
Week 2: Elements of design 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Identify line, shape and space in digital products. · Lesson 1
  2. Investigate texture, colour, form and tone using small visual studies. · Lesson 2
  3. Modify a supplied design to change its intended message. · Lesson 3
  4. Annotate the effect of selected elements on the audience. · Lesson 4
Week 3: Principles of design 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Explain balance, emphasis and dominance using contrasting layouts. · Lesson 1
  2. Use proximity and repetition to build unity and pattern. · Lesson 2
  3. Investigate movement and explain the intended viewing sequence. · Lesson 3
  4. Apply elements and principles to a campaign poster and justify changes. · Lesson 4
Week 4: Typography and composition 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Compare typeface, size, formatting and spacing for readability. · Lesson 1
  2. Construct layouts using a grid and consistent alignment. · Lesson 2
  3. Explore reading gravity and the rule of thirds in a visual composition. · Lesson 3
  4. Review a peer’s layout and revise typography and composition. · Lesson 4
Week 5: Computer components and storage 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Identify the input, processing, output and storage needs of a media task. · Lesson 1
  2. Explain the CPU’s role in a computer system. · Lesson 2
  3. Distinguish primary and secondary memory or storage using a workflow. · Lesson 3
  4. Recommend peripherals for a simple media-production brief. · Lesson 4
Week 6: Operating systems and compatibility 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Compare desktop, mobile and server systems for different users. · Lesson 1
  2. Compare Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and Linux in a supplied scenario. · Lesson 2
  3. Explain how an operating system manages resources, access and applications. · Lesson 3
  4. Check hardware requirements and justify a compatible hardware/software choice. · Lesson 4
Week 7: Graphic and audio formats 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Distinguish vector and raster graphics through scaling experiments. · Lesson 1
  2. Compare CGM, SVG, BMP, GIF, JPEG, TIFF and PNG for intended outputs. · Lesson 2
  3. Compare MP3, WAV and WMA using an audio-production scenario. · Lesson 3
  4. Choose graphic and audio formats for a campaign asset pack. · Lesson 4
Week 8: Organising tools and assets 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Design folders, filenames and an asset register for a client project. · Lesson 1
  2. Investigate software installation and update decisions in an assigned environment. · Lesson 2
  3. Compare open source, closed source, proprietary, shareware and freeware licensing. · Lesson 3
  4. Organise an asset pack and explain how the structure serves the client. · Lesson 4
Week 9: Spreadsheet lookup foundations 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Construct a readable spreadsheet table for campaign resources. · Lesson 1
  2. Model exact-match VLOOKUP using a vertical reference table. · Lesson 2
  3. Model exact-match HLOOKUP using a horizontal reference table. · Lesson 3
  4. Choose and test lookup formulas with known and missing values. · Lesson 4
Week 10: Sorting, filtering and spreadsheet layout 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Sort related records without breaking row relationships. · Lesson 1
  2. Use filters to answer questions about campaign resources. · Lesson 2
  3. Improve spreadsheet composition, labels and layout for a client. · Lesson 3
  4. Complete an independent spreadsheet practice task and correct errors. · Lesson 4
Week 11: Attribution and publication decisions 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Investigate the syllabus concepts of fair dealing, private use and moral rights. · Lesson 1
  2. Distinguish ownership, permission and acknowledgement in publication scenarios. · Lesson 2
  3. Create consistent references for a small set of digital assets. · Lesson 3
  4. Review a campaign asset pack and correct missing source information. · Lesson 4
Week 12: Digital citizenship and communities 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Compare social-networking types and their features. · Lesson 1
  2. Explore online chat rooms and virtual worlds as communities. · Lesson 2
  3. Analyse cyberbullying scenarios and propose management strategies. · Lesson 3
  4. Explain responsible participation and the effect of technology on work-life balance. · Lesson 4
Week 13: Plan the campaign project 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Analyse the campaign situation, audience and presentation medium. · Lesson 1
  2. Create a style guide linked to the agreed design criteria. · Lesson 2
  3. Generate thumbnail alternatives and a storyboard or wireframe. · Lesson 3
  4. Review the design plan and annotate the selected approach. · Lesson 4
Week 14: Produce and review the campaign 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Create the first campaign product from the design plan. · Lesson 1
  2. Prepare supporting graphic or audio assets in suitable formats. · Lesson 2
  3. Check the product against the criteria and collect user feedback. · Lesson 3
  4. Refine the product and explain changes using evidence. · Lesson 4
Week 15: Evaluate and consolidate 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Evaluate the product against each criterion and record limitations. · Lesson 1
  2. Demonstrate the product and explain the design and application choices. · Lesson 2
  3. Complete a short-answer practice task across the unit. · Lesson 3
  4. Use feedback to correct misconceptions and improve an extended response. · Lesson 4
Week 16: Revision and exam readiness 4 × 55 minutes
  1. Retrieve design concepts and annotate an unfamiliar product. · Lesson 1
  2. Review hardware, formats and spreadsheet decisions in a new scenario. · Lesson 2
  3. Practise an integrated client response under teacher-set timed conditions. · Lesson 3
  4. Review answers and prepare an evidence-based revision plan. · Lesson 4

Weeks 17–18: exam reserve. No extra teaching lessons are scheduled here.
