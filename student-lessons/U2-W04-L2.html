2. Understand the lesson ideas

A business needs files that are small enough to share, clear enough to use and easy to recover. Keep a suitable master, create delivery copies and make changes traceable.

Choose maintenance for the actual problem

Disk clean-up and temporary-file or browser-cache controls can reclaim space, but may remove useful downloads, offline content or sign-in state if the wrong options are chosen. Inspect the categories and follow the school’s approved procedure. Fragmentation means parts of a file are stored in separate locations; traditional hard-drive defragmentation can reduce the resulting mechanical access work. SSDs have different behaviour. Use the operating system’s drive-aware optimisation rather than treating every drive like a hard disk.

3. Follow a worked decision

A fictional bicycle shop needs a small communication solution, managed files and a justified LAN proposal. Staff and customers have different access needs. Use disposable working copies and invented customer information.

Today’s decision in that case

HDD fragmentation and SSD maintenance differ. Follow drive-aware OS guidance rather than applying one operation to every drive.

  1. Identify: Which requirement, concept or distinction does this decision address?
  2. Explain: Which part of the reasoning supports the decision?

The decision is a supplied teaching model. Its proposed checks are not your observed results.

Deeper chapter model: Choose a useful delivery copy
  1. Brief: A fictional shop needs an online product image. Its label must remain readable on the target screen.
  2. Master: Keep the original 2400 × 1600 image in the source folder.
  3. Copy A: 1200 × 800, 180 kB; label readable.
  4. Copy B: 600 × 400, 45 kB; label unreadable. These are supplied example observations, not measured exports.
  5. Decision: Choose A from these two options because B fails the readability criterion.
  6. Record: Save the settings and approved version, then check the actual uploaded result.

File size is one constraint. The required information still has to survive the export.

More support or challenge · choose if needed

Spot and correct a connected misconception

Claim to examine: A synced folder guarantees a recoverable backup.

Compare the correction

Changes and deletions can synchronise too. Check version retention, independent backup and the restore procedure.

Supported chapter question

A student reduces both image dimensions by half and predicts that the JPEG file will be exactly one quarter of its previous size. What is sound, and what needs checking?

Check the supported reasoning

The pixel count becomes one quarter: half the width multiplied by half the height. The encoded JPEG size must be measured because compression and image content also affect it.

Related practice and explanation

Distinguish

A crop removes the shop’s opening hours. Is a smaller file enough to justify it?

Compare an explained answer

No. The output has lost required content. Change the crop or another setting while preserving the communication goal.

Plan

Two staff members edit separate copies called final.docx. What would improve control?

Compare an explained answer

Agree a shared editing location, responsibilities and version identifiers. Record approval and recovery options rather than relying on the word final.
